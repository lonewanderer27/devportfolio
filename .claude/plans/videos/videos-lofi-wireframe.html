Videos section – Reel grid wireframe v2.12

Simplified to an Instagram-style grid: only the video previews are shown, in a fluid grid that fits as many columns as make sense for the screen (see C2). Everything else (category, title, description, results, tools) now lives in the modal, which puts the video on the left at full height and the details on the right. Try the filters, hover a thumbnail to preview it, click it to open the modal, and use the arrows. New: the hover preview with a sliding outline (B2) and the mobile long-press pop-out (E9). Move between thumbnails to see the outline travel while the blur stays on and the previous video fades out while the next one starts right away. Opening a clip now grows the thumbnail into the video (B3). All motion is summarised in B4.

Thumbnail to video transition

MomentWhat happens

Different shapesVertical clips match the thumbnail ratio, so they grow cleanly. Square and wide clips widen as they grow and the poster is revealed in its full shape at the end

Reduced motionNo movement at all: the modal and pop-out appear and disappear instantly

C2

Grid – columns by screen size

fluid: as many columns as fit, within a size range; not a fixed 5
RuleSpec
Thumbnail widthNever narrower than ~104px on phones, growing gradually with the screen to a ceiling of ~180px minimum on large displays. Thumbnails fill the leftover space evenly, so they end up roughly 105 to 190px wide.
ColumnsAs many as fit at that minimum width. The count rises step by step as the screen widens and never jumps by more than one.
SpacingPage padding 16px on phones, 36px elsewhere · gap 4px on phones, 6px elsewhere
Maximum widthContent stops growing at 1400px and stays centred, so very wide displays show more margin, not tiny thumbnails.
Last rowLeft-aligned and left incomplete; no stretching.
CSS idearepeat(auto-fill, minmax(clamp(104px, 72px + 8vw, 180px), 1fr))

Mobile – long-press preview

press and hold a thumbnail: it pops out in the centre like an Instagram post, autoplays muted, and the rest of the page blurs. Try it on the first phone (mouse or touch).
TopicRule
GesturePress and hold a thumbnail for 450ms. A quick tap still opens the full viewer (E3)
While holdingThe thumbnail shrinks slightly (93%), dims a little, and a ring fills in its centre. Where supported, a light haptic tick fires when the pop-out opens
Pop-out cardCentred on screen, about 70% of the screen width, in the clip’s own shape (9:16, 1:1 or 16:9) with the title in a slim header above. Rounded, with a soft shadow. Autoplays muted from 0:00 as a loop; thin progress bar and 🔇 badge
Blurred pageEverything else blurs (about 7px) under a 45% white veil, fading in as the card scales up from the thumbnail
ReleaseLifting the finger closes the pop-out: the card shrinks back into its thumbnail (about 400ms, see B3) while the blur fades out. No extra buttons, nothing to tap
CancelMoving the finger more than 10px, or starting to scroll, before 450ms cancels the press, so scrolling is never hijacked
Private clipsThe pop-out plays only the public part, never past the 🔒 timecode
Data saver / reduced motionThe pop-out shows the still poster with no autoplay
System menuThe browser’s own long-press menu (save image, copy) is turned off on thumbnails
Assistive techThe pop-out is an extra, never the only route: double-tap on a thumbnail opens the full viewer with all controls
