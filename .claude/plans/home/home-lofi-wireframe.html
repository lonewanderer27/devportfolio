Selected work – card spec

the three cards, one by one: parts, measurements, motion and content
Rules shared by all three cards
TopicSpec
LayoutDesktop: row 1 holds Documents and Videos side by side (equal width, 20px gap); row 2 holds Brand collaterals across the whole row. Order is fixed: Documents, Videos, Brand collaterals
Card surfaceSoft grey (#eee), 14px radius, 1.5px dark outline (same as the page cards), 30px padding, 300px high. Every card is text on the left and content on the right in the same line
Text columnAbout 170px wide: title (28px extra-bold), one line of grey text (14px), then a pill button. Nothing else is written on a card
ButtonPill, 1.5px ink outline, white fill, 12.5px, “View all … →”. Does not wrap
Click targetThe text side and the “View all …” button link to the full page (Documents, Videos or Brand Collaterals). Each item in the visual on the right is clickable and opens that item in a viewer on top of the Home page (see Open viewer)
Open viewerClicking (or pressing Enter on) a book, a film frame or a collateral piece opens the same viewer as on its own page, with the dim backdrop over Home: the Documents page reader (pages, numbered pagination, locked pages), the Videos page player (play, scrub, locked clip request, next up) or the Brand Collaterals type-aware modal (brochure spread, flyer, newsletter, voucher flip). ✕, Esc or a click on the backdrop closes it and returns to Home exactly where it was; the Home motion is paused while it is open. On phones the viewer fills the whole screen
HoverPointer cursor. Each card’s visual reacts a little (see its own table). Moving motion pauses while the pointer is over the card
MotionDocuments changes every 3.5s and Videos every 4s. They are not synced, so the row never moves in step. Collaterals is still
Reduced motionNo automatic changes, no transitions; the first item stays on show
PhonesCards stack one per row, full width, still text left and content right (smaller). Under 620px the content drops under the text
Content sourceEverything shown is the owner’s picks from the Documents, Videos and Brand Collaterals pages. No new content is made for Home

PartSpecNotes
1Progress crumbsFour crumbs across the top of the card: Availability › About you › Your need › Confirm. On phones only the current name shows, with the numbered dots
2Step 1 · AvailabilityOn desktop and tablets: calendar on the left, times on the right (a hint until a day is picked); “Next” stays disabled until a time is chosen. On phones (800px and below): the contact section shows only a heading and a “Check availability” button (no card, no progress crumbs yet), which opens the scheduler in a full-screen modal. Choosing a time is required in both; there is no skip optionFree days are outlined, fully booked days struck through and striped, unavailable days faded
3Step 2 · Introduce yourselfName and email, one under the other. Inline error in plain words if emptyOptional company name can be added here later
4Step 3 · What do you need help with?The three service chips plus “Something else” (multi-select), then “Tell me more” box. At least one chip is requiredSame service names as What I do
5Step 4 · Confirm the detailsA summary card: Time, Name, Email, Help with, Message, each row with an “Edit” link that jumps back to that step. One filled “Confirm my interest ↗” buttonNothing is sent before this button
6Back / Next“Back” (outline, left) and “Next” (filled, right) in a footer under a thin line. Step 1 has no Back. Step 4 replaces Next with ConfirmEnter submits the step; Esc does nothing
After sendingCard is replaced by “Thanks, Jane. Interest received.” with the chosen time and “I’ll confirm by email”All four crumbs turn black
Phones – scheduler modalTapping “Check availability” opens a full-screen modal (covers the whole screen, no page behind it). Header: ✕ and “Check my availability”. Body: the calendar, legend and, once a day is picked, the time pills (large tap targets). Footer, pinned: “Confirm date & time” (disabled until a time is picked; a time is required)✕, Esc or the Back button close it without choosing; the page stays on the button
Phones – after confirmingThe modal closes and the 4-step card appears at step 2 (“Introduce yourself”) with the chosen time shown at the top (“Tue 6 Oct · 10:30 · Change”). Change, or Edit on step 4, reopens the modal with the old day and time selected. Steps 2 to 4 are one screen each with Back / Next pinned at the bottomHero and nav “Book a call” buttons scroll to step 1
