# Plan: Design, redesign according to the Design System (Documents page and shared header)

Ticket DPD-034 (Low, User Interface), area *Design*: *As a visitor, the Documents page and the site header look and behave the same, and well, on a phone, a tablet, a laptop and a large desktop, and follow `.claude/plans/design-system.html` and `documents-hifi-wireframe.html`.*
Branch suggestion `feat/DPD-034-Design-Redesign-According-To-Design-System`. **Theme only**; no plugin change, no version bump. Written after the work was done, so it records what changed and why, in the final state (several first attempts were replaced; they are listed under *Tried and replaced*). Builds on `DPD-002-003-025-Header-Categories.md`, `DPD-016-Shelf-Grid.md` and `DPD-023` (the mobile Details sheet that still shows the tools).

**Status:** everything below is implemented in the working tree and checked (theme 325, plugin 726 with 12 skipped, modal JS 148, all passing); **committed on the branch, not pushed**. Parts of the *Reference page for future pages* section are agreements for later tickets, and are marked as such.

**Intent:** the Documents page is the **reference page**. Videos, Collaterals, Services and the Home sections will be modelled after it, so what is decided here (tokens, font, header, the archive page pattern, the filter and the shelf) is meant to be reused, not copied. See *Reference page for future pages* below.

**Scope note:** the ticket started as a polish pass on the Documents page (CSS, font sizes, small fixes) and grew to the **shared header**, so the navigation changes below affect every page.

**Out of scope (follow-ups):** how tools are shown under a book on phones and a checkbox filter (`DPD-035-Reusable-Checkbox-Filter.md`), the singular category name on the cover (`DPD-036-Book-Category-Make-The-Category-Name-Above-Each-Document-Singular.md`), the modal and mobile viewer visuals other than their font, and site-wide heading weights.

## How it was reviewed
Rendered `https://devportfolio.ddev.site/documents/` in headless Chrome (Google Chrome, plus `puppeteer-core` for scrolling, clicking and device emulation) at 360, 375, 390, 700/780, 820, 900, 1024, 1280, 1440 and 1920px, at the top of the page, scrolled, with the phone menu open, mid category change, and in landscape. Phone widths used device emulation or a same-width iframe because headless Chrome's window cannot go below about 500px. Compared against the design system tokens and the hi-fi wireframe (`.chipbtn`, `.page-title`, `.shelf`, `.tags`, `.nav-desk`, `.nav-phone`, the mobile `.chiprow`). Three reports from the owner's real phone (a gap above the header, the menu icon, pills and books sliding over the navbar) were reproduced and fixed.

## A. Documents page

1. **Phone grid.** Phones around 360 to 375px wide got one giant cover per row (the 150px column floor fits two columns only from 390px). At 480px and below the shelf is `repeat(auto-fill, minmax(max(110px, calc(50% - 16px)), 1fr))`: the floor is just under half the width, so two columns always fit and three never do, without a fixed `repeat(2, ...)` (an existing test forbids fixed counts).
2. **Smoother tablet step.** The 820px-and-below floor is 170px (was 150px), so about 780 to 820px shows three columns instead of jumping from three to four; below about 620px it shows two wider columns.
3. **Category pills follow the design system chip** (were 13px, 11px on phones, 19 to 28px tall, no hover): 14px, weight 500, `min-height: 36px` (13px and 34px at 820px and below), `white-space: nowrap`, an invisible `::after` tap area reaching 44px, a 160ms colour transition and a `--fill` hover on unpressed pills only for mouse users (`@media (hover: hover)`). The 1.5px ink outline and the pressed state are unchanged.
4. **Phone category strip** (sticky, one scrolling row): it bleeds to the screen edges (negative margin and matching padding from `--documents-gutter`, `clamp(0px, 3vw, 32px)`, set on `.documents-page`, plus the 24px root padding) instead of clipping pills at the page gutter; it sticks right under the header (`top: --site-sticky-top + --site-header-height`), has the same frosted glass as the header (the shared glass tint and blur tokens, 80% white and `blur(20px) saturate(180%)`) and the shared soft edge line as its bottom line. On a phone **held sideways** (`orientation: landscape` and `max-height: 520px`) it scrolls away instead of stacking under the sticky header (about 30% of the screen); a short *portrait* screen keeps it sticky.
5. **Page title matches the hi-fi `.page-title`**: class `documents-page__title` on the template heading, `clamp(44px, 6.4vw, 72px)`, weight 200, `-0.04em`, line-height 1, margin `0 0 28px`.
6. **Phone goal: as many books as possible on the first screen.** At 820px and below the page's top padding is 16px (was 48px, set inline by the template, so it is overridden with `!important`), the title is `clamp(36px, 9vw, 52px)` with a 12px gap. At 480px and below the shelf's top padding is 16px, row gap 20px and the info block's top padding 10px, and the **software tags are hidden under each book** (they wrapped to three lines; the tools are still in the modal and the mobile Details sheet; DPD-035 revisits how tools are shown). At 390px wide the first cover starts at 254px (was about 440px) and the second row at 608px: a 750px-tall browser shows the first row fully and about 60% of the second, and 4 full covers need about 860px. Showing 4 full covers on a 700px screen needs a shorter cover (2:3 is 245px tall at this width), which is a design decision left open (a 3:4 phone cover would do it).
7. **One hairline above the shelf on phones and tablets.** At 820px and below the shelf's own top border is removed and the strip's gap is gone, so the strip's line is the only one (two lines about 25px apart looked doubled).

## B. Font

8. **Hanken Grotesk everywhere**, the design system family, **self-hosted** so nothing is requested from Google: `assets/fonts/hanken-grotesk-{200,300,400,500,600,700,800}.woff2` (latin subset, about 14KB each, about 100KB in all), declared as `fontFace` in `theme.json` with `fontDisplay: swap`, fallback `'Helvetica Neue', Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`. The `sans` and `serif` presets are the same family. The three Georgia and `system-ui` rules in `document-modal/style.css` now use `var(--wp--preset--font-family--sans)`. Other headings keep the theme's global 800 weight (the design system uses lighter headings; a site-wide change is left to the owner).

## C. Header (shared by every page)

9. **Navigation follows the design system `.nav-desk` / `.nav-phone`.** Links are ink, weight 500, 14px, `6px 12px` padding, 34px tall (`box-sizing: border-box`, otherwise padding made them 46px), 6px radius, `2px` gap (was 22px and wrapped "Contact" onto its own line at about 700px). Hover is a `fill` background. The **active page is a filled ink pill** with light text (was a grey link with a 2px underline). Header padding is 12px top and bottom (was 16px). A new `.site-header__bar` group holds the site title and the navigation.
10. **Sticky with iOS 7 frosted glass** (design system "materials"). `header.wp-block-template-part` is `position: sticky`, `z-index: 20`, with `top: var(--site-sticky-top)`. The glass is its `::before`: `--wp--custom--glass--tint` (80% white) and `--wp--custom--glass--filter` (`blur(20px) saturate(180%)`; item 16) (72% made black covers blur to a dirty grey; 88% hid the blur). The blur is on the pseudo-element, never on the header: a `backdrop-filter` there would become the containing block of the fixed phone menu overlay and collapse it. Opaque white when `prefers-reduced-transparency` or `prefers-contrast: more` is set, or when `backdrop-filter` is unsupported. `:root` has `--site-header-height: 61px` (bar `min-height: 36px` plus 12px padding twice plus the 1px line); `html` has `scroll-padding-top` so `/#services` style anchors clear the header.
11. **Admin bar gap (found on the owner's phone).** The WordPress admin bar is `position: fixed` only above 600px; below it scrolls away, but its `--wp-admin--admin-bar--height` was still reserved, leaving a transparent 46px gap above the header. The offset is now `--site-sticky-top`, equal to the admin bar height only at `min-width: 601px` (header, category strip and `scroll-padding-top` all use it). Visitors who are not logged in were never affected.
12. **Pills and books sliding over the navbar on a category change.** The pill group and every book have a `view-transition-name`, and their snapshots paint above everything, including the sticky header. The header takes the name `site-header` **only while the change runs**: `document-categories/view.js` adds the class `is-view-transitioning` before `startViewTransition()` and removes it when `transition.finished` settles (also when skipped). The CSS keeps the header static in the transition (`animation: none`, old state hidden) with a solid white backing, because the blur is not captured in the snapshot. The name cannot be permanent: any `view-transition-name` makes the element a backdrop root and **switches the glass blur off** (found when the owner reported the text behind the header staying sharp).
13. **Phone header and menu.** Under 600px the site title sits on the left and the menu button on the right. The button is a 36px outlined square with a 44px tap area; core's two-line icon is hidden and **three centred bars are drawn in CSS** (18px x 2px at 10, 17 and 24px, as the design system's `.burger`). In the full-screen phone menu the links are 18px with 44px tap targets, centred in a pill with 16px padding, and the active item is a light `fill` with ink text (the overlay sets its own text colour, so a dark pill had invisible text). The overlay still opens full height.
14. **Quieter separators.** The header's bottom line is plain CSS on `header.wp-block-template-part > .wp-block-group` (the inline border was removed from `parts/header.html`) and, like the strip's, is `--wp--custom--edge` (`rgb(0 0 0 / 0.1)`, the design system's `--edge`) instead of `#c9c9c9`. On a **portrait phone** (`max-width: 820px` and `orientation: portrait`) the Documents page drops the header's line, so the sticky header and the pill strip read as one frosted block with a single hairline. The dotted shelf zone and the planks are untouched.
15. **Site title on large screens, Contact on the right.** From 1024px the bar is a three-column grid (`1fr auto 1fr`: title, centred links, an empty spacer column) so the links stay centred, and the header is 1440px wide so the title lines up with the page title. Between 600px and 1023px the title stays hidden (the seven links fill the row). From 1024px **Contact** (still the seventh link in the markup) is pinned to the right edge as an outlined pill (1.5px ink border, fully rounded, `display: flex` so the label centres), balancing the title; below 1024px it is a normal link. It is positioned against the bar, so the nav wrappers that core makes `position: relative` are set to `static` there (with `:not(.is-menu-open)` so the phone overlay is untouched).
16. **Shared glass and edge values live once in `theme.json`.** `settings.custom` defines `--wp--custom--glass--tint` (`rgb(255 255 255 / 0.8)`), `--wp--custom--glass--filter` (`blur(20px) saturate(180%)`) and `--wp--custom--edge` (`rgb(0 0 0 / 0.1)`). The header's glass layer and bottom line, the phone category strip and the blur on the modal's round arrow buttons read them; the block stylesheets keep the literal as the `var()` fallback so they still work if the property is missing. Checked on the live page: the computed values and the look are unchanged. Deliberately **not** promoted: the modal arrows' 72% tint and the local `--shelf-*`, `--book-*`, `--st-*` and `--dm-*` tokens (their existing tests pin the literals, and no second page needs them yet; see the timing rule below). While adding it, the two separate `.documents-shelf` rules inside the shelf's 480px query were merged into one (no behaviour change).

## Reference page for future pages (intent)
This ticket is also where the site's look is settled. The next pages (`/videos/`, `/collaterals/`, the Services archive, then the Home sections) should be built **from the Documents page**, so the same decisions are not made twice and the pages do not drift apart. Nothing below is built yet except where marked *done*; it is the agreement for the follow-up tickets.

**What a new page inherits as is (done in this ticket):**
- **Header and navigation:** one shared part (`parts/header.html`), sticky frosted glass, filled active pill, Contact pinned right from 1024px, title on phones and large screens. A new page adds nothing; its nav link only needs the archive to resolve through the existing `devportfolio_prepare_navigation_link` filter.
- **Font and type scale:** Hanken Grotesk from `theme.json` (`var(--wp--preset--font-family--sans)`); no block names a font family or a system font. Page titles use the Documents title recipe (`clamp(44px, 6.4vw, 72px)`, weight 200, `-0.04em`, smaller on phones); body 14px; small labels 10 to 13px uppercase with letter spacing.
- **Shape, colour and edge:** the `theme.json` palette (`base`, `contrast`, `mid`, `line`, `fill`), 6px radius on controls, pill-shaped chips (36px, 34px on phones, 44px tap area), soft hairlines and glass from the shared tokens (`--wp--custom--edge`, `--wp--custom--glass--tint`, `--wp--custom--glass--filter`, item 16).
- **Layout tokens:** `--documents-gutter`, `--site-header-height`, `--site-sticky-top` (the admin-bar rule), the 1440px wide page (`--wp--style--global--content-size`) and the phone rules (16px top padding, two columns, tags hidden, 36px title).
- **Behaviour rules:** the pill strip is sticky under the header on portrait phones and static when held sideways; a view transition on a filter change names the header only while it runs (`is-view-transitioning`); every transition and animation honours `prefers-reduced-motion`.

**The page pattern to copy (archive page = title + filter + shelf + viewer):**
1. A template `templates/archive-<post-type>.html` with the same skeleton as `archive-document.html`: the `main` group with the `<name>-page` class and the title class, a content group, the filter block, the shelf block and (when there is a viewer) its modal block. Same inline padding, same `layout: constrained`, same 1440px override.
2. The same four blocks, in the same order: **filter** (category pills), **shelf** (grid of items with a plank per row and the dot-grid zone), **item** (the cover or thumbnail and its info) and **viewer**. Videos need a 9:16 or 16:9 thumbnail item and a video viewer; Collaterals reuse the document viewer; Services may be a simpler list. The shelf grid rules (170px floor, two columns on phones, one hairline) and the info block (description clamped to two lines on phones) are the contract.
3. A filter that follows the same URL pattern (`?category=<slug>`, `pushState`, `popstate`, view transition, reduced motion) and the same shelf contract (`[data-<name>-shelf]`, items as direct children, planks owned by the script).

**Timing rule: extract when the second page is built, not before.** The goal is to make more of the Documents page reusable (generic filter, shelf and item blocks, the shared filter and plank module, more shared tokens), but **only when we implement the other pages** (Videos, Collaterals, ...). Until then the Documents blocks stay document-specific and untouched. Reasons: a generic version designed from one example guesses wrong (a video item or a service list will show what really varies), the current 325 theme tests and the DPD-002 to DPD-016 contracts stay valid, and no unused abstraction has to be maintained. So each item below is **planned, not scheduled**: the ticket that builds the second page does the extraction as its first commits (refactor with the Documents tests unchanged and green, then add the new page on top), and records it in its own plan. Only values that are already shared by more than one block today (the glass tint, the blur and the soft edge line) have been promoted so far.

**What has to change in the code before a second page can use it (the shared tokens are done; the rest are follow-up tickets):**
- **Generalise the document-specific blocks (when the second page is built).** `document-categories`, `document-shelf` and `document-book` hard-code `document`, `document_category` and `data-documents-shelf`. Extract a taxonomy-agnostic filter block and a post-type-agnostic shelf, keeping the Documents markup and classes (or documenting the rename) so the existing 325 theme tests keep guarding it. The shared view-transition and plank code moves to one module that the filter, the shelf and the new checkbox filter (DPD-035, built to be reusable) all use.
- **One home for the tokens (done for what is already shared; the rest when a second page needs it).** `theme.json` `settings.custom` now holds the values every page shares: `--wp--custom--glass--tint` (`rgb(255 255 255 / 0.8)`), `--wp--custom--glass--filter` (`blur(20px) saturate(180%)`) and `--wp--custom--edge` (`rgb(0 0 0 / 0.1)`). The header (its glass layer and its bottom line), the phone category strip and the modal's round arrow buttons read them (blocks keep the literal as the `var()` fallback). The tint of the modal arrows (72%) and the local cover and shelf tokens (`--shelf-*`, `--book-*`, `--st-*`, `--dm-*`) are intentionally left as they are; promote more only when a second page needs them, so a change is made once without breaking their tests.
- **Shared item data.** The item info (title, description, software tags), the singular category name above a cover (DPD-036) and the software tags block are already written to work for any post type that stores `cptdp_software_tools` (document, video, collateral); new pages should reuse them rather than add fields.
- **Keep the tests as the specification.** `tests/test-design-system.php` pins the header, the font and the page recipe. A new page adds `tests/test-<page>.php` for its own structure and extends `test-navigation.php` only for a new link; it must not weaken the design system tests.

**Checklist for a new page (add to `AGENTS.md` conventions):**
- [ ] Template copied from the Documents archive, with `<name>-page` and the title class.
- [ ] No font family, no hard-coded colours that exist as palette entries, no fixed pixel breakpoints other than 480, 600, 820 and 1024 (the ones used here).
- [ ] Filter, shelf and item use the shared blocks or the generalised versions, never a new copy.
- [ ] Checked at 360, 390, 820, 1024, 1440 and 1920px, logged in and out, portrait and landscape, with the phone menu and a filter change.
- [ ] Tests added; theme, plugin and modal suites green.
- [ ] `AGENTS.md` and the ticket plan updated in the same change.

**Documents to keep in step:** this plan (the record of the decisions), `AGENTS.md` (the architecture and conventions, now noting the Documents page as the reference and the test file), `.claude/plans/design-system.html` and `documents-hifi-wireframe.html` (the source of truth for look; when a page diverges from them on purpose, record it here), and each later ticket's plan, which should start by saying which Documents page parts it reuses.

## Tried and replaced
- Software tags as one line with ellipses at 480px and below: in a 150px column "Notion, Google Workspace, +1" cannot fit and "Not..." looked worse than wrapping. Replaced by hiding the tags on phones (item 6); the leading dot on a wrapped line on tablets still matches the hi-fi.
- `repeat(2, minmax(0, 1fr))` for phones: an existing test forbids fixed column counts; replaced by the half-width floor (item 1).
- A permanent `view-transition-name` on the header: fixed the overlap but switched the blur off (item 12).
- A glass tint of 72% (dirty grey over black covers) and 88% (blur barely visible); settled on 80%.
- The strip's landscape rule on `max-height: 520px` alone also hid the sticky strip on short portrait screens; it now needs `orientation: landscape`.
- A blanket `min-height: 36px` header link rule without `box-sizing: border-box` (46px tall pills) and a header gap of 22px (wrapped "Contact").
