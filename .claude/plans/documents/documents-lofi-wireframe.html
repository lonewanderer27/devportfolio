TopicRule
Shelf columns≤ 480px: 2 · 481–820px: 3 · 821px and up: 4 (proposed; current wireframe uses 2 below 820px)
ViewerFull-screen on phones; centred modal from ~700px wide
Page layoutSingle page fit-to-width below 700px; two-page spread above (portrait multi-page documents)
GesturesSwipe / edge-tap to turn; double-tap or pinch to zoom; swipe down on the header to close
Locked pagesSwipe stops at the first locked page with a bounce; “Pages” grid shows 🔒; form in a bottom sheet
Back buttonCloses the viewer (and sheets) instead of leaving the portfolio
Touch targetsMinimum 44 × 44px for buttons, chips and the page-grid tiles
PerformanceLazy-load pages and thumbnails; low-res first, sharp on zoom
MotionRespect “reduce motion”: swap slide / bounce for a simple fade
Notes
  • Covers carry no “Open / Request” tag; the difference only appears once a book is clicked.
  • Open books adapt to the document: portrait + multi-page = two-page spread (single page on mobile); landscape = one wide page; single portrait page = one centred page. Arrows and numbered thumbnails turn pages, with a progress bar and page counter.
  • Locked documents are not blocked up front: the viewer opens normally, the Next button stops at the first locked page, locked pages are marked with a 🔒 on their thumbnail, and only when the visitor turns to one does that page show the warning with a name + email request form. Which pages are locked is a content decision for Dave.
  • No intro line or sticky “Hire me” bar, per earlier feedback. Titles/tools are placeholders.
