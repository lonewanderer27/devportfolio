# Documents page

Categories, book, shelf and the document modal, incl. its mobile sheets and deeplink.

- `blocks/document-categories/` — dynamic block `devportfolio/document-categories` (DPD-002/003), registered in `functions.php` on `init`: `block.json`, `render.php`, `style.css`, `view.js`; attributes `taxonomy` (default `document_category`) and `label` (default translated "Document categories") make the same filter reusable for `collateral_category`, while unknown taxonomies render nothing and `view.js` remains a no-op without a shelf. Sits in `archive-document.html` under the title. "All" is synthetic (always first, never stored, `data-filter=""`, `aria-pressed="true"` by default); then one `<button>` per `document_category` term in name order (the DPD-007 seeded order), empty terms included, `data-filter` = term slug. On screens <=820px the pills become 44px rows in a dropdown: the trigger shows the active plural category, uses `aria-expanded`, and opening is not a history entry; the list is closed on selection, outside/focus leave, Escape, and growing past the breakpoint. `view.js` keeps one pill active and filters in place, syncing the trigger label in `apply()`. The pill group and the site header get their `view-transition-name` only while a change runs (class `is-view-transitioning`): a permanent name makes an element a backdrop root and switches off the blur of its frosted band. The active category lives in the URL as `?category=<slug>` (All = no param): `pushState` on click, `popstate` re-applies on Back/Forward, `render.php` marks the matching pill pressed on first paint, and an unknown slug falls back to All (bad param cleaned with `replaceState`). Switches run inside `document.startViewTransition()` so the pill group (`view-transition-name: documents-categories`) fades in 0.2s; the root transition is disabled, and unsupported browsers or `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` apply the change instantly. Shelf contract for DPD-016: shelf `[data-documents-shelf]`, each item `data-document-categories="slug slug"` (direct child of the shelf), planks `[data-shelf-plank]` are owned by the script (rebuilt after every filter and resize, one per row of grid columns). The group is sticky under the sticky site header at every size (`top: --site-sticky-top + --site-header-height`, relative with `top: auto` on a short screen held sideways), on a full-width frosted band (`::before`, shared glass and edge tokens) that is the only line above the shelf. Mobile (<=820px): the dropdown replaces the scrolling strip, and the group itself is the glass band. `apply()` also dispatches `devportfolio:category-change` (bubbling, `detail: { slug }`) synchronously on every change, including the first; the collateral grid listens for it.
- `blocks/document-book/` — dynamic block `devportfolio/document-book` (DPD-005/006/008), registered in `functions.php` next to the categories block: `block.json` (`usesContext: ["postId"]`), `render.php`, `style.css`. Renders the **cover only** (`div.documents-book[data-cover-style]`): `__cat` (first `document_category` term by name, empty span if none), `__ttl` (title; the short ornament line is its `::after`), `__ft` holding `__format` (`devportfolio_document_format_label()`: `PDF · 24 pp`, `1 p` for one page, parts dropped when missing) and `__owner` (`DAVE`). No status tag, description or tags on the cover; renders nothing for non-`document` posts. Cover style comes from post meta `cptdp_cover_style` (registered by the plugin, picked in the document's details meta box from `cptdp_document_cover_styles()`, which must list the same slugs in the same order), whitelisted by `devportfolio_sanitize_cover_style()` against `devportfolio_cover_styles()` (`midnight`, `pinstripe`, `horizon`, `split-sky`, `dot-grid`, `two-tone`, `certificate`), unknown or missing = `midnight`. 2:3 cover following `.claude/plans/documents/documents-hifi-wireframe`: fixed light-mode `--book-*` tokens from `.claude/plans/design-system.html`, each style sets `--cv` / `--ct` / `--cat-c` (Midnight, Horizon, Split Sky and Two-Tone are dark with white text; Pinstripe, Dot Grid and Certificate are light with dark text) and its pattern in the `__pat` layer (`<i aria-hidden>`, first child); only Horizon carries the gradient (the sun). Certificate (DPD-043) is white with fine guilloche rings (`repeating-radial-gradient`, ink at 8%) centred on its seal, Midnight's hairline frame (same inset, ink at 24%) and a small line-art award seal with ribbons (inline SVG data URI, no image file) on `__pat::after`, top right where Horizon has its sun; it keeps the shared padding and left-aligned text, so its category, title and footer line up with the other books. 16px spine and a dashed stitch inset 8px top and bottom; at `max-width: 820px` the smaller two-column sizes apply. Motion is CSS only: hover (`@media (hover: hover)`) lifts 6px, tilts 1deg and deepens the flat shadow over 260ms; `:active` scales to 0.985 (what a tap shows); reduced motion disables both. The cover is not interactive: the shelf item (DPD-016) wraps it in the button/link and owns focus and click.
- `blocks/document-shelf/` — dynamic block `devportfolio/document-shelf` (DPD-016), registered in `functions.php`, placed after the categories block in `archive-document.html`. `render.php` outputs `div.documents-shelf[data-documents-shelf]` with one `article.documents-item[data-document-categories]` per published document (menu order, then title; the block renders nothing without documents): the `document-book` cover, then `__info` with the title (`h3` holding the one `button.documents-item__open[data-document-id]`, whose `::after` covers the whole item so the item opens the modal), the description (`devportfolio_document_description()`, omitted when empty) and the software tags (the reusable `devportfolio/software-tags` block, rendered with the item's `postId`). Tags are text separated by a gradient dot (hi-fi `.tags`, same as the modal header); a full-width dot grid with top and bottom lines sits behind the shelf (`.documents-shelf::before`); items rise in (360ms) unless the browser supports View Transitions; on a category change the filter runs in `document.startViewTransition()` and every item has its own `view-transition-name` (`documents-item-<id>`, inline) and every plank one per row (`documents-plank-<n>`, set by `renderPlanks()`), class `documents-item`, so books glide to their new spot or fade in and out over 0.4s (`::view-transition-group(*.documents-item)`; none with reduced motion or without support); and because the stretched button covers the cover, the cover hover lift and pressed state are driven from the item; the `+N` tag is `display: none` above 480px (see the software tags block). Grid: `repeat(auto-fill, minmax(min(100%, 200px), 1fr))`, `170px` at 820px and below, and at 480px and below `max(110px, calc(50% - 16px))` so a phone always gets exactly two columns, so the number of books per row follows the available width (2 on a phone, 5 at 1440px, 6 at 1920px: the Documents page is up to 1440px wide with the root side padding plus up to 32px, set in the shelf CSS on `.documents-page`, instead of the theme's 1040px) instead of fixed 4/3/2 steps (a deliberate change from the ticket); at 480px and below the description is clamped to 2 lines and only the first 2 tags show plus `+N`. With the Documents setting `show_book_title` off (the default), the shelf gets `documents-shelf--hide-titles`: the title text is not drawn (`font-size: 0`) but the button stays, named by its `aria-label`. Planks are created by `document-categories/view.js` and styled here (`.documents-shelf__plank`). Helper `devportfolio_document_shelf_item()`.
- `blocks/document-modal/` — dynamic block `devportfolio/document-modal` (DPD-009/010/011/012/014/015), registered in `functions.php`, placed once in `archive-document.html`. `render.php` outputs one shared native `<dialog class="documents-modal">` (header, blank body for DPD-013, footer) plus a `<script type="application/json" class="documents-modal__data">` payload of every published document (`devportfolio_document_modal_payload()`: id, title, description, tools, format label, pages, download mode/URL). `view.js` (script module, imports `pager.js`) opens it on a click of any element with `data-document-id="<postId>"` (the `document-book` cover carries it; the DPD-016 item button must too), only when `(min-width: 701px)`; closes on backdrop click (click target is the dialog), Esc, the Close button, or shrinking to 700px or less. Header: serif bold title, one-line description (post excerpt, else first 20 words of the content), software pills, one `PDF · 24 pp` pill, Download link (`<a download>`; mode `file` = attachment URL, `embed` = embed URL in a new tab, `none` = `aria-disabled`), Close X button (last control of the same flex row as Download, same 36px height, so both align; Esc is the native `<dialog>` behaviour). Footer (hidden at <=700px): pager (Back, numbers, Next; <=9 pages all numbers, else 1, last, window of 3 each side or 2 in the two-page view, a one-page gap shows the page; visible pages filled; Back/Next `aria-disabled` at the ends; hidden for 1 page) and a thin progress line on the footer's top edge (no visible text: its `aria-valuetext` carries `Pages 11–12 of 24` / `Page 5 of 24`; the whole footer is hidden for documents with fewer than 2 pages). Two-page view for a portrait document with `pages > 1` (always starts on an odd page) **while the spread's pages are at least 80% of the width one page could have** (`spreadIsWorthIt()` and `SPREAD_MIN_SCALE` in `layout.js`, used by the viewer for mouse devices; touch devices keep the stricter `spreadFits()`): in a tall, narrow window the viewer drops to one larger page instead of leaving bands of empty grey above and below a small spread. Below 1100px wide the header is a grid: title and description (two lines) with the format pill, Download and Close on the right of the same row, and the software tools on their own row underneath. Page arrows (DPD-014): round Previous/Next buttons in the body, vertically centred, same `turnPage()` as the pager, `aria-disabled` + dimmed at the ends, hidden entirely for documents with fewer than 2 pages, placed by `--dm-arrow-inset` and sized by `--dm-arrow-size` on the body (18px/38px, 10px/34px at 1100px and below, 8px at 900px and below; BF-DPD-038), shown only while hovering the body (`@media (hover: hover)`; always visible without hover, and on keyboard focus). The body holds the arrows and an empty `.documents-modal__stage`. The body's padding steps down with the window so the document gets the room (it is measured from the stage, so it grows by itself): `30px 76px` on large screens (the 76px side gutters keep the arrows clear of the page), `12px` and smaller arrows at 1100px and below, `0` at 900px and below (the arrows float over the page edges), and the vertical padding goes at 700px tall and below. State `{ id, page, step, pages }` is dispatched as `documents:modal-change` on the dialog for DPD-013 to fill `.documents-modal__stage`. `pager.js` is pure and tested with `node --test blocks/document-modal/tests/pager.test.mjs` (run from the block directory; Node 24 in DDEV: `ddev exec --dir=/var/www/html/wp-content/themes/devportfolio/blocks/document-modal node --test tests/pager.test.mjs`).

**Viewer body (DPD-013):** `viewer.js` fills `.documents-modal__stage` with an StPageFlip open book (vendored `vendor/page-flip.browser.js`): normally from stored images (payload `images`, `ratio`, `imageWidth`; pdf.js is never downloaded), and only when none are valid from pdf.js rendered in memory (module/worker URLs from `data-pdfjs`, supplied by the plugin), guarded by `capability.js` `clientRenderTier()` (feature detection, version floors Chrome/Edge 119, Firefox 124, Safari 17.4 matching pdf.js 4.10.38, data saver, `deviceMemory`, `pdfBytes`/page limits, 7-day `localStorage` failure flag, tiers `full`/`light`/`refused`; limits overridable through the `devportfolio_document_viewer_limits` filter into `data-limits`). Refused or failed = 'can't be previewed', Download still works. Stored-image books with a payload `cropBottom` get class `documents-modal__book--cropped` and `--documents-crop` on the book: each image is `100% / (1 - crop)` tall, pinned to the top, and the page clips the empty strip (payload `ratio` is already for the visible part; the page number stays at the bottom of the visible page). The pdf.js fallback is NOT cropped. With `selectable_text` on (`data-selectable-text`), `viewer.js` tries pdf.js FIRST (page = canvas + `.documents-modal__text` layer built with `pdfjs.TextLayer`, `--scale-factor` = the page's CSS px per PDF unit), turns StPageFlip's mouse events and corner hints off (`useMouseEvents: false`, class `documents-modal__book--selectable`) so dragging selects text and pages turn by arrows, pager and keys, and falls back to the stored images (not selectable) when pdf.js is refused by the gate or fails and images exist. `layout.js` (`fitPage`, `orientationOf`) and `pager.js` `stepFor( pages, orientation )` are pure. Portrait with 2+ pages = spread (odd last page alone on the left), landscape or 1 page = single view. JS tests: `ddev exec --dir=/var/www/html/wp-content/themes/devportfolio/blocks/document-modal 'node --test tests/*.test.mjs'`.

**Presentations (DPD-026):** `mode.js` (pure, `node --test`) holds `viewerMode( doc )` (`native` > `images` > `pdf` > `unavailable`) and `canDrawPresentations( env )` and the wording helpers (`unitOf`, `wording`, `pieceLabel`, `positionText`). The payload gains `kind` (`pdf`/`pptx`/`ppt`/`odp`/`other`, from `devportfolio_document_file_kind()`, core functions only) and `previewUrl` (the plugin's `cptdp_document_browser_preview_url()` through `function_exists`); presentations have no `viewerUrl` (no pdf.js, no selectable text) but get their stored slides like a PDF. Slide wording: `devportfolio_document_format_label( $filetype, $pages, $kind )` gives `PPTX · 12 slides` for presentations (cover and modal pill), and `data-units` on the block root (`devportfolio_document_unit_strings()`) feeds `Slide 3 of 12`, `Previous slide` and the pager label. Converted slides use the normal book (a landscape deck is a single wide page per view).

**Native viewer for PowerPoint:** with a `nativeUrl` (the plugin's `cptdp_document_native_pptx_url()`), which wins over the stored images, `viewer.js` `showPptx()` loads the bundled viewer with `import( PPTX_LIB )` only for that document (`vendor/ooxml/`, `@silurus/ooxml` 0.89.7, MIT: a Rust/WebAssembly parser and a Canvas 2D renderer, about 6 MB of the package's 21 MB vendored by following the import graph of `pptx.mjs`, plus `pptx_parser_bg.wasm`; see `vendor/ooxml/README.md` for what is left out and how to update) and draws one slide on a canvas with a transparent text layer, so text can be selected and copied; hyperlinks, media, element selection and web fonts are switched off, so it requests nothing but the file itself. If it cannot draw the file (no WebAssembly, error, timeout) `showPptx()` returns false and the stored slides are shown, or "can't be previewed" when there are none. It needs WebAssembly, `Worker` and a 2D canvas (`canDrawPresentations()`), times out after `limits.nativeTimeoutMs` (30 s) and falls back to "can't be previewed". The modal's own pager, arrows, keys and progress line turn the slides (`goTo()` calls `goToSlide()`, `isTurning()` is true while one is being drawn), the canvas is scaled with CSS to fit the stage, and `destroy()` frees the workers on close.

**Zoom (BF-DPD-038):** the round zoom buttons (`showZoomFab()`, shared with the Excel and Word native viewers) call the library's `zoomIn()`/`zoomOut()`, which redraws the slide and sets the canvas's inline width and height; `showPptx()` stores the scale it opened at, and while the scale differs (`isZoomed()`, re-checked through the library's `onScaleChange`, passed through `openPptx()`) `fit()` leaves that size alone and toggles `__native--zoomed` on the wrapper and `__stage--zoomed` on the stage, so the slide scrolls over the whole body (the stage turns `position: static`, the wrapper is `inset: 0` against the body, its child centred with `margin: auto`, the canvas `max-width` clamp lifted); `reset()` clears it. The zoom buttons are appended to the body, not the stage (so they do not move when a zoom starts), centred on the Next arrow's column and as wide as the arrow (`--dm-arrow-inset`, `--dm-arrow-size`). It does not cover `.ppt` or `.odp`, equations (MathJax is not vendored) or some charts and effects, and has no animations. 'Can't be previewed' has a presentation hint (`data-hint-slide`). The host must serve `.mjs` as JavaScript and `.wasm` as `application/wasm` (DDEV does) and should compress `application/wasm` (1.99 MB raw, 0.75 MB gzip; DDEV does not), see `vendor/ooxml/README.md`. `pptx.mjs` and the wasm are requested with `?v=${PPTX_VERSION}` (keep it equal to the vendored version; a test checks), the canvas is drawn at the size it is shown (about 2.6 MP at 1100 px on a retina screen, 10 MB), and a first open costs about 22 requests and 555 KB gzip plus the wasm, with the first slide in roughly 200 ms. The pager keeps the keyboard focus on the control you used when it redraws, so the arrow keys keep working after clicking a page number. The payload title is plain text (`html_entity_decode()`), as `textContent` writes it. Tests: `tests/test-document-presentations.php` (`Devportfolio_Test_Document_Presentations`) and `blocks/document-modal/tests/mode.test.mjs`. Browser check used for DPD-026: headless Chrome (puppeteer-core) against `https://devportfolio.ddev.site/documents/`; there is no browser test runner in the repo.

**Spreadsheets in the modal (DPD-027):** `devportfolio_document_file_kind()` also returns `xlsx|xls|ods` and `devportfolio_document_is_spreadsheet()` tells them apart; converted spreadsheets stay in **page** wording (`XLSX · 3 pp`), only the native viewer is new. The payload (`devportfolio_document_viewer_data()` and the modal payload, one branch for slides and sheets, `nativeUrl` picked from `cptdp_document_native_pptx_url`/`xlsx_url` by kind) gives them their stored pages (no `viewerUrl`) and `nativeUrl` from `cptdp_document_native_xlsx_url()`. `viewer.js` `showXlsx()` (chosen when `doc.kind === 'xlsx'` and the mode is `native`) loads `vendor/ooxml/xlsx.mjs?v=OOXML_VERSION` with `import()` only for that document and mounts the library's own `XlsxViewer` grid (scroll, zoom, sheet tabs, cell selection and copy) in `.documents-modal__grid`, with `enableHyperlinks`, `comments`, `useGoogleFonts` and element selection off, `hiddenSheetMode: 'skip'`, `xlsxWorksheetLimits` from `limits.nativeMaxCells`/`nativeMaxRows` (2,000,000 cells, 200,000 rows) and the 30 s timeout. It reports one piece to `onGeometry()`, so the footer pager, arrows and progress line stay hidden; `view.js` leaves arrow keys to the grid. Failure or an unsupported browser (`canDrawOffice()`, the new name of `canDrawPresentations()`) shows the stored pages, or "can't be previewed" with the hint for the document (`hintKey`: `data-hint-slide`, `data-hint-sheet` or `data-hint`). `show()` dispatches with `( doc.kind === 'xlsx' ? showXlsx : showPptx )`, and `destroyNative()` frees any native viewer. `PPTX_VERSION` is now an alias of `OOXML_VERSION`; the vendored folder holds both engines (the six files without a content hash are requested with `?v=`). Tests: `tests/test-document-spreadsheets.php` (`Devportfolio_Test_Document_Spreadsheets`) and `blocks/document-modal/tests/mode.test.mjs`. Browser check: headless Chrome (puppeteer-core) against `https://devportfolio.ddev.site/documents/`.

**Mobile modal (DPD-017/018/019):** `render.php` adds a second `.documents-mobile` dialog. `mobile.js` owns its header/footer state, while `shared/download.js` shares download wiring with the desktop modal and `pager.js` exposes `mobileFooterParts()`. The mobile body is the mobile viewer (see below); the dialog emits `documents:mobile-change`, `documents:mobile-pages` and `documents:mobile-details`. Switching dialogs (crossing 700px, or rotating a touch screen, see `routing.js`) swaps with a View Transition using `data-documents-swapping` and the shared `view-transition-name: documents-modal`.

**Deeplink (DPD-032):** the open document lives in the URL as `/documents/?document_slug=<slug>` (next to `?category=`; `document_slug` avoids WordPress's `document` CPT query-var collision; other args and the hash are kept). The modal payload carries `slug`. `blocks/shared/deeplink.js` (pure, `node --test tests/deeplink.test.mjs`) has `slugFromUrl()`, `urlWithSlug()`, `urlWithoutSlug()`. `view.js` opens a known slug on first load (the mobile modal when `routing.js` says mobile: 700px or less, or a touch screen held sideways; DPD-017, DPD-024; the shelf button gets the focus back on close), drops an unknown or unpublished slug with `replaceState`, `pushState`s on a click open, steps back with `history.back()` on close when the modal added the entry (else `replaceState` removes the param), and `popstate` opens or closes it without pushing; Back closes the mobile modal. Published `/documents/<slug>/` single URLs redirect to `/documents/?document_slug=<slug>` at `template_redirect`, so the archive opens the matching modal; drafts and non-document singles are not redirected. Plan: `.claude/plans/documents/DPD-032-Document-Modal-Deeplink.md`.
**Mobile Pages sheet (DPD-020):** the `Pages` button (footer, and the round button in touch landscape) opens a bottom sheet inside the mobile dialog (`blocks/document-modal/pages-sheet.js`, loaded with `import()` on the first tap; a pure model plus a thin builder, built once per document and updated in place on reopen). Gallery (the default the first time) is 3 columns, at most 3 rows visible (scrolls), List is one row per page with the name in bold and `current` after the current page's name (both pages of a spread are current); with no name a row shows `Page`/`Slide`/`Sheet` only. Thumbnails load **only when visible** (`data-src` plus one `IntersectionObserver` on the sheet's scroll area, about one row ahead, nothing in List view, nothing above the open page, none with data saver; `thumbs` reaches the page as a `{ base, ext, count, width, height }` descriptor and `pageNames` as ranges, both omitted when empty); with no thumbnails the tile is a numbered blank page. The sheet **remembers, per document and for as long as the page stays open** (in memory, not stored; it survives closing and reopening the viewer), the **view last used** and **where each view was scrolled to** (`createSheetMemory()`, written just before the sheet hides; a long document is rebuilt as far as it was scrolled; a view never scrolled before is centred on the current page). Opening pushes one history entry (`sheet-back.js`, helpers in `deeplink.js`) so Back closes only the sheet; its `popstate` listener **stops the event** for the moves the sheet owns, because WordPress's Interactivity Router (loaded for the Navigation block) answers every `popstate` and **reloads the page** for a URL it has not cached (it only caches the URL the page loaded with, so `?document_slug=...` and `?category=...` reloaded on the first Back/Forward onto them). The modal's own Back/Forward handler (`view.js`) stops the event whenever it opens or closes the modal (and on the sheet's stale entry), and the category filters' handler (`document-categories/view.js`) does nothing unless the category actually changed (a View Transition there snapshotted and re-animated the whole modal) and keeps a real category change from the router. **Listeners run in the order they were added** (capture does not run first on `window` here), so these three are registered as their scripts run, ahead of the router, which is imported later: do not move them into `DOMContentLoaded` or a lazy import. Checked in headless Chrome at 390x844 with 11 Back/Forward flows (0 reloads); Esc closes the sheet first. The **handle (and the title row) can be dragged down to dismiss** (pointer events with capture; past 96px, or a flick of 0.6px/ms after 24px, it slides out and closes, otherwise it springs back; a tap or Enter/Space on the handle also closes; reduced motion skips the animation; `dragOutcome()` is pure and tested). Plan: `.claude/plans/documents/DPD-020-Document-Modal-Mobile-Pages-Sheet.md`; backend plans: `.claude/plans/backend/DPB-030-Document-Generate-Page-Names.md`, `DPB-031-Document-Generate-Page-Thumbnails.md`.

**Mobile Details sheet and shared sheet components (DPD-023):** the footer `Details` button and the round Info button both emit `documents:mobile-details`; `mobile.js` opens the **Details sheet** (`blocks/document-modal/details-sheet.js`, loaded with `import()` on the first tap): a bottom sheet over the dimmed viewer with the title (serif, bold) and an X, the category (outlined pill), description, software pills (no headings, as in the lo-fi wireframe F2b), `Format` and `Pages` rows (`Slides` for a presentation, row left out when it has no value) and a full-width dark Download button pinned at the bottom (`applyDownload`/`guardDisabledDownload` from `download.js`). Closes by X, dim tap, drag down, Esc or Back. Held sideways it **stays open on rotation** and re-lays out in two columns (text scrolls on the left, rows and Download on the right; CSS only, the same markup). Content rules are the pure `detailsModel()` (tested with `node --test`). The payload gains `category` (plain text) and `fileType`; `devportfolio_document_category_name()` (also used by the cover) and `devportfolio_document_file_type_label()` (also used by the format line) are the one source for both. **The old details popover (`documents-mobile__details-panel`, `toggleDetails()`, the FAB's `aria-expanded`) was removed**; the FAB now has `aria-haspopup="dialog"` and the viewer takes an `onDetails` callback like `onPages`, and no longer stores document metadata. **Shared sheet components** (refactored out of the Pages sheet; do not copy them for a new sheet): `bottom-sheet.js` `createBottomSheet()` is the shell (dim layer, sheet and ARIA, handle, header row, scrolling body, `inert` viewer, Esc and the dialog's `cancel`, drag to dismiss with the pure `dragOutcome()`/`dragOffset()`; the shell never closes itself, it calls `onDismiss`; controls in the header that keep their own click carry `data-no-drag`); `sheet-controller.js` `createSheetController()` registers sheets by name, loads each module once, allows one open at a time, owns the single history entry through `sheet-back.js` (its `popstate` listener is created when `mobile.js` builds the controller, never inside a lazy import) and has a per-sheet `closeOnRotate` (Pages `true`, Details `false`). `pages-sheet.js` now holds only the Pages content. A sheet dismissed by the visitor now also pops its history entry (before, only a choice or Back did). Plan: `.claude/plans/documents/DPD-023-Document-Modal-Mobile-Details-Sheet.md`. Tests: `tests/test-document-modal-mobile-details.php` and `blocks/document-modal/tests/{details-sheet,sheet-controller,bottom-sheet}.test.mjs`.
