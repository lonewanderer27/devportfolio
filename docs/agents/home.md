# Home page

Hero, contact, stats, services, Selected work, opening animation and the Home anchors.

- `blocks/hero/` — dynamic block `devportfolio/hero` (DPH-001, DPH-003, DPH-004), registered in `functions.php`, placed first in `templates/front-page.html`. The name is the Hero title from Settings > Contact (first word outlined, the `h1`; `devportfolio_hero_title()`), the portrait is the stored attachment (`devportfolio_hero_portrait_id()`, preloaded in `wp_head`; `devportfolio_hero_portrait_sizes()` is the one sizes string), roles come from `devportfolio_hero_roles()` (the highlighted Job Role on top, the others in order) and the links from Settings > Contact. Desktop and tablets (621px and up) use the absolute layout with `--hero-h` (the window below the sticky header on desktop, half the viewport on tablets); phones (620px and below) use the lo-fi wireframe's mobile layout. `view.js` (`viewScript`) hides the "Scroll down" hint for the rest of the visit once the visitor scrolls (a refresh shows it again). Entrance motion is CSS only, inside `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference` and 621px and up. Tests: `tests/test-hero.php` (`Devportfolio_Test_Hero`).
- `blocks/contact/` — dynamic block `devportfolio/contact` (DPH-025 to DPH-028), placed last in `templates/front-page.html`, section `#contact` (the nav's Contact link). `render.php` outputs the four step forms, the phone dialog and a JSON payload (`devportfolio_contact_config()`); `calendar.js`, `steps.js` and `time.js` are pure (`ddev exec --dir=/var/www/html/wp-content/themes/devportfolio/blocks/contact 'node --test tests/*.test.mjs'`); `picker.js` is the calendar and times, shared by the card and the phone modal; `view.js` keeps state in memory only and creates `createSheetHistory()` while the module runs. The 800px query is `MOBILE_QUERY` in `view.js` and the same in `style.css`. Strings come from `devportfolio_contact_strings()`. Tests: `tests/test-contact-block.php`.

**Home page (DPH-002, 005, 006, 007, 008, 009, 029):** `templates/front-page.html` (its `<main>` has the shared `documents-page` shell from `page.css`, so its width and side gutters match the other pages; hero slot first, then the stats strip, then the "What I do" section `#services`). `blocks/stats-strip/` (`devportfolio/stats-strip`): the first 3 published Statistics by menu order then title, value = post content as plain text (tags stripped), label = title, plain `<ul>`, no link or button, 3 columns at every width. `blocks/service-list/` (`devportfolio/service-list`): one row per top-level published Service (menu order, then title), number from the position (`01`), benefit = content as plain text, tags = child Services as chips (1px light outline, 12px radius, grey text, white fill, not links, per the home lo-fi wireframe), no arrow, no lines (rows separated by padding and a hover tint); the rows are plain text with no link (the Services nav link still points to `/#services`); tags hidden at 640px and below. `blocks/site-footer/` (`devportfolio/site-footer`) feeds `parts/footer.html`. Tests: `test-site-footer.php`, `test-stats-strip.php`, `test-service-list.php`, `test-home-template.php`. Plan: `.claude/plans/home/DPH-002-005-006-007-008-009-029-Home-Nav-Stats-Services-Footer.md`.

**Home Selected work (DPH-010 to DPH-021):** `blocks/selected-work/` is the dynamic block `devportfolio/selected-work`, placed in `templates/front-page.html` under "What I do" (group `#selected-work`, `h2` "Selected work") followed by the existing `devportfolio/document-modal`, `devportfolio/collateral-modal` and `devportfolio/video-modal` blocks, which open from any element carrying `data-document-id` / `data-collateral-id` / `data-video-id` (the video viewer has no tile on Home, so it uses its plain fade instead of the fly-out). What it shows is **the posts ticked "Mark as highlight (Selected work)"** (`cptdp_highlight`, `devportfolio_work_picks( $post_type, $limit )`, published, Order then title, no fallback): up to 4 documents, 5 videos and 6 collaterals. Three cards in a 2 + 1 grid (one column at 1000px and below; the card is `fill` grey, 1.5px ink outline, 14px radius, 30px padding, 300px high, `auto` high and stacked at 620px and below). Text side: `devportfolio_work_card_text()` (title and line are one link, the pill is a second link to the same archive).

**Documents:** a fan of books (`.work-docs`, the Documents cover drawn at 200px and scaled to 150 x 225px above 1200px, 112 x 168px from 621px to 1200px, 170 x 255px on phones (150 x 225px at 360px and below), centred in its row; roles `front`/`right`/`left`/`hidden` in `data-role`, side books 7 degrees, 40px out (26px from 621px to 1200px); the fan is shifted left by `--work-shift` (63px, 43px from 621px to 1200px, 27px margin on phones) so its right-hand book ends on the card's 30px padding line, the same as the other two cards, `brightness(0.82)`, only the front book is not `inert`; each book is a `button.work-docs__open[data-document-id]` laid over the cover, the hover lift is driven from the item).

**Videos:** a film strip (`.work-strip`, `#222` band with sprocket holes and a mask, frames up to 99 x 176px (`--fw` is `min(100px, 100cqi / 3.2)` of the strip's column, so the band fills the column; on phones the reel spans the whole card and `--fw` is `calc(100cqi / 2.8)`), roles `active`/`prev`/`next`/`far-prev`/`far-next`; each frame has `data-length`, `data-preview-src` only for a clip with a stored preview clip or file, and a `button.work-strip__open[data-video-id]` over it; only the active frame is not `inert`).

**Collaterals:** `.work-glimpse`, one `button.work-piece[data-collateral-id]` per piece at its real ratio (`--ratio`), `devportfolio_work_piece_columns()` stacks a ratio >= 1.8 strip under the strip before it, at most 5 columns, from 621px to 900px (iPad Mini size) only the first three highlighted pieces show (`.work-piece--extra` and `.work-glimpse__col--extra` are hidden) and the height is `min(216px, (100cqi - 32px) / 2.4)`; base height `--glimpse-h` is `min(216px, (100cqi - 64px) / 3.72)` of the column (the pieces are packed to the right with 16px gaps and fill the column width; 84px at 620px and below). The text column is 200px on Documents and Videos and as wide as its title and line on Brand collaterals above 1000px, so the collateral title and line stay on one line. Motion lives in `view.js` with the pure `rotation.js` (`BOOK_PERIOD` 3500, `FRAME_PERIOD` 4000, `bookRole()`, `frameRole()`, `tick()`) and `time.js` (`timeLabel()`, `fraction()`): a clock counts only while the card is on screen, not hovered (mouse and pen), without keyboard focus-visible, the tab visible and no `<dialog>` open; reduced motion starts nothing; the script builds the only `<video>` (muted, looping, inline, no controls) for the middle frame once the card is visible, and never with data saver. First-paint roles are written by `devportfolio_work_book_role()` / `devportfolio_work_frame_role()`, which a test keeps equal to `rotation.js`. Each card title has a `view-transition-name` (`work-title-documents`, `work-title-videos`, `work-title-collaterals`, in `blocks/selected-work/style.css`) and the matching archive title has the same name (`body.post-type-archive-<type> .documents-page__title` in `assets/css/page.css`). `page.css` also opts both pages into cross-document view transitions (`@view-transition { navigation: auto; }`), so the title morphs (0.6s) when going Back from an archive to Home. Going forward, Chrome does not attach the transition to a freshly loaded page, so the move is the page cross-fade only (Chrome and Edge 126+, Safari 18.2+; reduced motion turns it off). Plan: `.claude/plans/home/DPH-010-011-012-013-014-015-016-017-018-019-020-021-Home-Selected-Work.md`.

**Home sections opening animation (DPH-032):** every Home section after the hero fades in and rises 14px once, the first time 15% of it is on screen (elements that arrive together are staggered by 80ms, at most 5 steps; elements on screen at load animate on the first paint; scrolling back never replays it). One mechanism for all of them: elements carry the class `home-reveal` (in `templates/front-page.html` the What I do, Selected work and Software tools headings and the intro; each `stats-strip__item`, `service-list__row` and `work-card`; the testimonials heading, stage and dots; `ul.software-tools`; the contact heading, lead, phone button and card; the `site-footer` block, inert on other pages). `assets/js/home-reveal.js` is printed inline at the top of `<head>` on the front page only (`devportfolio_print_home_reveal_script()`, `wp_head` priority 1): with IntersectionObserver and without reduced motion it adds `has-home-reveal` to `<html>`, then, once parsing has ended (`readystatechange`, not DOM-ready, which waits for every script module), gives each element `is-revealing` with `--home-reveal-index` and, when the animation ends, `is-revealed` (nothing left on the element); a script error, or parsing longer than 3 s, removes the class, and a page entered through the cross-document view transition (`pagereveal` with `viewTransition`, Back from an archive) removes it at once so the Selected work title morph is untouched. `assets/css/home-motion.css` (front page only, `devportfolio_enqueue_home_motion()`) is one `@media screen and (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)` block: the start state and the `home-reveal` keyframes, `opacity` and `translate` only (no layout change: anchors land where they did, no layout shift). Tokens: `theme.json` `settings.custom.motion` (`ease` `cubic-bezier(.2, .8, .2, 1)`, `reveal-duration` 520ms, `reveal-distance` 14px, `reveal-stagger` 80ms; `--wp--custom--motion--*`). Rules: never put `home-reveal` on the hero or on a wrapper that holds a dialog, a fixed or sticky element or a backdrop blur (the `#selected-work` group holds the three viewers, `section.contact` holds the fixed phone card, `section.testimonials` holds the dot grid); the phone contact card (`.is-modal > *`) is excluded and keeps its own `contact-modal-in`; an element with focus inside is never hidden. A new Home section opts in by adding the class to its heading and items. Tests: `tests/test-home-motion.php` (`Devportfolio_Test_Home_Motion`). Plan: `.claude/plans/home/DPH-032-Home-Sections-Opening-Animation.md`.
Testimonials and Contact are Home-page sections, linked as anchors `/#testimonials` and `/#contact`; the Home page sections need matching `id`s. Anchor links are never marked active.
