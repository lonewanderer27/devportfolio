# Videos page

Video grid and video modal, incl. motion.

- `blocks/video-grid/` — dynamic block `devportfolio/video-grid` (DPV-006/007/008), registered in `functions.php` after the collateral modal and placed in `archive-video.html` after the categories block. `render.php` outputs `div.videos-grid[data-videos-grid]` with one `article.videos-item[data-video-categories]` per published video (menu order, then title). The item's one control is an empty `button.videos-item__open[data-video-id]` named by the title; the poster is the featured image, shown 9:16 and centre-cropped, a blank tile when there is none. `data-preview-src` (plus `data-preview-clip` for the stored 6 s clip) is set only for a video with an uploaded file; embed-only videos never preview. The grid is pure CSS: `repeat(auto-fill, minmax(clamp(104px, 72px + 8vw, 180px), 1fr))`, 6px gap (4px at 620px and below). `view.js` filters on `devportfolio:category-change`. `preview.js` plays a muted preview in place after 800 ms of hover (mouse and pen only; never with reduced motion or data saver): the stored clip loops itself, an uploaded file without a clip is looped at 6 s in script, and a progress bar and a mute badge appear. While one plays the rest of the page is veiled (7px blur under a 40% white veil, `body.is-videos-previewing`). Moving to another tile switches at once and keeps the veil; leaving the grid ends it. Category changes animate the tiles with View Transitions: each tile has its own `view-transition-name` (inline, from `render.php`), and the category block runs the filter inside `document.startViewTransition()`. Helpers in `functions.php`: `devportfolio_video_item()` and `devportfolio_video_preview_url()`. No videos renders `p.videos-empty--none` ("No videos yet.") and the category pills are hidden with `:has()`; the sibling `p[data-videos-empty]` after the grid is toggled from `filter()` through `visibleCount()` ("No videos in this category"). A clip the editor chose (`cptdp_video_preview`) is used like the generated one (DPV-040).
  - Motion (DPV-030 to DPV-038): the veil layers are 30 (`body::after`, above the header 20), playing tile 31, outline 32 (`outline.js`, a `.videos-outline` slid by `morph()`), pop-out 40. `preview.js` ends a previous video with the `is-fading` class (150 ms, `FADE_MS`). `longpress.js` (touch, mobile routing only, 450 ms hold, 10 px tolerance) opens `popout.js` (a card that plays the 6 s loop, or shows the still poster under reduced motion, data saver or no file). Tiles carry `data-ratio` (the `aspect_ratio` term) and `videos-item--poster`; `view.js` sets `is-loaded`/`is-failed` for the skeleton. The grid has `data-hint` ("Release to close"). Node tests: `blocks/video-grid/tests/{outline,longpress,popout,preview}.test.mjs`. Browser check was done with headless Chrome (puppeteer-core in a scratch folder, not in the repo) on `/videos/`.
- `blocks/video-modal/` — dynamic block `devportfolio/video-modal` (DPV-009 to DPV-016, the desktop half of DPV-024), registered in `functions.php` after the video grid and placed once in `archive-video.html` after the grid. `render.php` outputs one desktop `dialog.videos-modal` (`data-pane` `wide` or `tall`; video pane left, 380px details pane right, nearly full height (`100dvh - 48px`); landscape (any ratio but 9x16) is a 640px media column beside the 380px details pane, as Instagram's post view, the video centred with bars as needed; portrait (9x16) is a 9:16 video at the modal's full height (423px wide at 800px tall), flush with the modal, no border) and a `script[type=application/json].videos-modal__data` payload with `order` (the grid's order) and `items` from `devportfolio_video_modal_payload()` (plain text and URLs only; the pane comes from the `aspect_ratio` term; an uploaded file wins over the embed, and only YouTube and Vimeo embeds are used, via `devportfolio_video_embed_src()`). `view.js` opens it on `[data-video-id]` on a 701px+ screen, fills it with textContent, plays the file or embed (closing unloads it), and shows Results, Made with, Next up (`nextItems()` with `sameCategoryFirst`, from the active `document-categories` filter) and the footer (Open original post, Copy link). Deeplink `?video_slug=<slug>` (`createDeeplink( 'video_slug' )`); a published `/videos/<slug>/` redirects to it. The mobile viewer (DPV-011, DPV-019 to DPV-023) is a second dialog, `dialog.videos-mobile`, that `shared/routing.js` gives a screen 700px wide or less or a touch screen held sideways; crossing that boundary with a viewer open swaps the dialog in place (same video and URL). `mobile.js` (`createMobileVideo()`) fills it and reads touches through `mobile-gestures.js` (pure: `classifyStroke()`, `tapAction()`); `neighbours.js` (pure) gives ▲ ▼ the previous and next video in the active filter; `details-sheet.js` (`videoDetailsModel()`, pure) is the ⓘ and title sheet, built on the shared `bottom-sheet.js` and `sheet-controller.js`; `view.js` owns the history (one entry per open, `video_slug` replaced on ▲ ▼, closing steps back, one capture-phase `popstate` listener). The player is split: `player.js` is the engine only (the `<video>` or embed, autoplay and the muted fallback, loop or replay-once, events) and every button is a module in `controls/` (`controls/index.js` is the registry; `mountControls()` mounts the keys a viewer is given; the desktop mounts `DESKTOP_CONTROLS`); `preferences.js` keeps mute, volume and speed for the page session in memory only. The mobile controls follow Settings → Videos: PHP renders markup only for the enabled keys and writes them to `data-mobile-options`. The Videos archive loads `devportfolio-viewer` (`shared/viewer.css`) for the shared sheet shell; `.videos-mobile` is in the `--dm-*` and `--vm-*` token scopes. Helpers in `functions.php`: `devportfolio_video_length_label()`, `devportfolio_video_ratio_slug()`, `devportfolio_video_shape_label()`, `devportfolio_video_embed_src()`, `devportfolio_video_modal_payload()`, `devportfolio_video_modal_strings()`, `devportfolio_video_single_redirect_url()`. Failure (DPV-027): `player.js` `fail()` sets `data-state="error"` (hides Play and the bar), shows `Try again` (`.videos-modal__retry`, remounts the video) and the `Watch on the original site` link when there is a post URL; an embed fails only when there is no source or the browser is offline. Desktop `←`/`→` step to the previous and next video of the active filter (`neighbours.js`, not the sliders). Under `@media (pointer: coarse)` the controls are 44px. `preload-next.js` loads only the next video's metadata while one is open, released on close (desktop and mobile). The player (DPV-017, DPV-018, DPV-029) is `blocks/video-modal/player.js` (`createPlayer()`, the DOM) over `player-model.js` (pure time, seek, replay and volume maths; node tests in `tests/`): an uploaded file plays in a native `<video>` without controls under a custom bar (play, scrub, time, mute, volume, speed, fullscreen); `fill( item, gesture )` decides the sound (a click starts with sound, a deeplink or Back/Forward starts muted, and a blocked start falls back to muted without changing the choice); mute, volume and speed are module variables for the page only (no storage); the gradient is used only for the scrub and volume fills and the playing frame; embeds get no bar and get `autoplay=1` only on a click.
  - Motion (DPV-033): a click flies the pane (`.videos-modal__video`) out of its tile with `shared/flight.js` (`frameOf`, `flyTo`; the same helper as the pop-out), the sidebar fades in beside it and the backdrop fades by CSS (`.videos-modal.is-morphing-modal`). Closing (`closeDesktop()`) flies it back into the tile (`is-leaving` fades the backdrop); with no tile it is a 160 ms `is-closing` fade, and reduced motion closes at once. `settleFlight()` lands a running flight (Esc, a press or a routing change). Deeplink and Back/Forward opens use the plain fade. No view transitions: they drew the dialog under the grid.
