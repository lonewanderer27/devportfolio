# Shared viewer

`blocks/shared/`, routing and the mobile viewer used by all three modals.

- `blocks/shared/` — JS and PHP that both the Documents and the Collaterals pages use (DPC-024). The viewer moved here (WP3, DPC-012 D3): `viewer.js` (`createViewer({ prefix, … onTap })`, the book, its native and Office viewers), `mode.js`, `native-pptx|xlsx|docx.js` and `vendor/` (StPageFlip, the OOXML bundle; `FLIP_SRC` and the `?v=` URLs resolve here), `pager-controller.js` (`createPagerController`, pure `footerModel()`), the page sources and generic modules, and the mobile modules `mobile-viewer.js`, `mobile.js` (`createMobileModal({ prefix, viewPrefix, eventPrefix, loadDetails })`), `sheet-controller.js`, `sheet-back.js`, `bottom-sheet.js`, `pages-sheet.js`. Class prefixes are arguments (default `documents-modal`/`documents-mobile`); `document-modal/` keeps the Documents-only parts (`view.js`, `details-sheet.js`, `render.php`, `style.css`). Also: `gestures.js` `tapSide()`, `shouldShowTapHint()`, `DOUBLE_TAP_WINDOW`. Other shared modules: `routing.js`, `deeplink.js` (`createDeeplink( param )`), `download.js` (moved from `document-modal/`) and `modal-trigger.js` (`bindOpen( scope, attribute, open )`, one click listener per page). `next-items.js` (`nextItems()`: the Next list of a modal, wrapping, restricted to the active filter, `sameCategoryFirst`, `minimum`) and `copy-link.js` (`linkFor( location, slug, param )`, `copyText()`) are shared by Collaterals and Videos (DPV-026). Helpers in `functions.php`: `devportfolio_page_count_label()`, `devportfolio_media_payload_base()`, `devportfolio_published_ids()` (use it instead of `get_posts()` for a published list), `devportfolio_term_slugs()` and `devportfolio_memo()` (per-request store, cleared by `devportfolio_memo_add_hooks()` on post, term and meta changes). The palette both modals share is `--wp--custom--modal--*` in `theme.json`. JS tests: `blocks/shared/tests/` (`node --test tests/*.test.mjs`).
  - Video motion (DPV-030 to DPV-038): `morph.js` is the one shared element transition (`morph()`, `settleMorph()`, `canMorph()`; `settle` runs the pending change, so a click or Esc mid-way lands on the end state). The hover outline is its only user. The pop-out and the desktop modal fly their elements with `shared/flight.js` (`frameOf`, `flyTo`, `flightDuration`: `element.animate`, size and place from the tile's box), so they stack above the grid.

**Mobile viewer (DPD-021 body, DPD-024 landscape and adaptive spread, DPD-029 presentations). PDFs and presentations:** Word documents are DPD-031 (below). `canShowOnMobile()` in `mode.js` is the one gate to widen (every known kind today: `pdf`, `pptx`, `ppt`, `odp`, `xlsx`, `xls`, `ods`, `docx`, `doc`, `odt`); an unknown kind shows "can't be previewed" with its hint (`hintKeyFor()`), and Download still works.

**Presentations (DPD-029):** converted slides go through the normal image source (landscape single view, slide wording, the rotate/pinch pills). A `.pptx` with Native viewer > PowerPoint on (`nativeAllowedOnMobile()`, `viewerMode() === 'native'`) is drawn from the original file by `showNative()` in `mobile-viewer.js`, through the mount shared with the desktop modal, `native-pptx.js` (`openPptx()`: library URLs with `?v=`, the privacy options, size and `dpr` caps, timeouts; `readNativeEnvironment()`): same limits as desktop (the plugin's 30 MB ceiling, `nativeTimeoutMs`), no mobile-only cap. The canvas is one element in the middle view of the normal frame, so gestures, zoom, hint, footer and round buttons are unchanged; a turn is `goToSlide()` in place (no slide, the canvas does not follow the finger), text is selectable by long press (`.documents-mobile__native` turns `user-select` back on), `reset()` frees the workers through `source.destroy()`, and any failure falls back to the stored slides or "can't be previewed". Plan: `.claude/plans/documents/DPD-029-Document-Modal-Mobile-PPTX-Viewer.md`. Plan and as-built notes: `.claude/plans/documents/DPD-021-024-Document-Modal-Mobile-Viewer.md`.

**Spreadsheets (DPD-030):** converted sheets use the normal page viewer (page wording, landscape pills). A `.xlsx` with Native viewer > Excel on (`nativeAllowedOnMobile()`, `isNativeScroll()`) is drawn by the library's own grid through `native-xlsx.js` (`openXlsx()`: the one place for the URLs with `?v=`, the privacy options and the worksheet limits, shared with the desktop `showXlsx()`; same limits as desktop, no mobile-only cap) in `showScroll()` of `mobile-viewer.js` (renamed from `showGrid()` in DPD-031). The grid is mounted **outside** the swipe/zoom frame in `.documents-mobile__scroll` (own scroll, pinch and cell selection: `touch-action: pan-x pan-y pinch-zoom`, `overscroll-behavior: contain`), so no page gestures, edge taps, hints or zoom buttons are built; it reports **one piece**, so the **footer and progress bar are hidden as on desktop** and the library's sheet tabs are the navigation. Only the round details button is kept (`makeDetails()`, shared with the page viewer), lifted above the library's tab bar by `--documents-grid-bar` (measured after mount; the stage gets `documents-mobile__stage--scroll`); on touch screens the bar is 40px (`:has( > .xlsx-tab-strip )`), held sideways the grid starts below the floating Close and Download (a Word document does not: it fills the whole screen and scrolls under the round buttons, so no bar shows across the top), and `mobile.js` leaves arrow, zoom and `0` keys to the grid. A failed grid falls back to the stored sheets or "can't be previewed" with the sheet hint; `reset()` destroys it. Plan: `.claude/plans/documents/DPD-030-Document-Modal-Mobile-XLSX-Viewer.md`.

**Word documents on mobile (DPD-031):** converted `.docx`/`.doc`/`.odt` pages use the normal page viewer (page wording, `DOCX · 12 pp`). A `.docx` with Native viewer > Word on (`nativeAllowedOnMobile()`, `isNativeScroll()`) is drawn by the library's `DocxScrollViewer` through `native-docx.js` (`openDocx()`: the one place for the URLs with `?v=`, the privacy options, `resourceLimits` and the best-effort `fitWidth()`, shared with the desktop `showDocx()`; same limits as desktop) in `showScroll()` of `mobile-viewer.js`, which also draws the workbook grid (the old `showGrid()`). Both are mounted **outside** the swipe/zoom frame in `.documents-mobile__scroll` (stage class `documents-mobile__stage--scroll`, one piece, no footer, no page gestures). A document adds `--doc` modifiers: no tab bar, so the details button keeps the normal inset, plus round zoom buttons wired to the library's `zoomIn()`/`zoomOut()`/`getScale()` (0.1-4). Failure falls back to the stored pages or "can't be previewed" with the document hint. Cleanup with this ticket: removed `canDrawPresentations`, `PPTX_VERSION` and `isMobilePdf`. Plan: `.claude/plans/documents/DPD-031-Document-Modal-Mobile-DOCX-Viewer.md`.
- **Which dialog a screen gets** is defined once in `shared/routing.js` (`createRouting()`): the **mobile** dialog at 700px wide or less **or on a touch screen held sideways** (`(pointer: coarse) and (orientation: landscape) and (max-height: 520px)`), the **desktop** dialog otherwise. `view.js` and `mobile.js` import it; `style.css` repeats the same two media queries (a node test, `tests/routing.test.mjs`, compares them); `view.js` `enforceSingleDialog()` closes the wrong one if both were ever open, so the two never overlap. Rotating or resizing while open swaps in place (same document, page and URL) inside a View Transition.
- **Body** (`mobile-viewer.js`, `createMobileViewer()`, mounted by `mobile.js`, which owns the page state and the footer): one page fitted to the width on light grey, or **two pages when the document is portrait and the space allows it** (`spreadFits()` in `layout.js`: 2+ pages and `stageW >= 2 * stageH * ratio`, re-checked on every resize, so rotation, folding and split screen re-lay out in place and keep the page). Turning is a slide that follows the finger; StPageFlip is never loaded on mobile. Sources are the desktop's (`page-source.js`): selectable text through pdf.js when Native viewer > PDF is on, stored images, pdf.js fallback behind `capability.js`; the bottom crop and the text layer are honoured.
- **Gestures** (pure maths in `gestures.js`, state in `touch-zoom.js`): swipe left/right turns; tapping the outer 20% of either side turns (8px dead strip at the screen edge); round previous/next hints sit on the edges and hide at the ends or while zoomed; double-tap (centre 60%) or pinch zooms 1x-4x, a drag pans a zoomed page, and swiping, edge taps and the hints are off while zoomed. Keys: arrows turn, `+`/`=`, `-`, `0` zoom (accessibility), announced in a polite live region.
-

**Hint:** `swipe or tap edges` floats under the document (a landscape document shows the pills `Rotate for a bigger view` and `Pinch to zoom` instead) and disappears at the first action in the body (any touch, turn, zoom or key).
- **Footer and round buttons.** Portrait with 2+ pages: footer (Pages, `3–4 / 24` for a spread, Details) and progress bar; no round buttons.

**One page:** the **footer and the progress bar are hidden** (supersedes DPD-019's "footer always shows"), and the round **details button** appears.

**Landscape (touch):** **no header and no footer**; Close floats top left and Download top right; a round **Pages** button sits above the round **details button** (bottom right); the previous/next hints, Close, Download and the round buttons share one 38px size and 12px side inset so they line up in columns; the progress bar stays for 2+ pages. The details button is shown whenever the footer is not (even for a document with no pages); like the footer's Details button it opens the **Details sheet** (DPD-023, below), the only place the title shows in landscape besides screen readers. The old popover is gone. The round buttons hide on a swipe down and return after a swipe up (or after 2 s). The mobile Pages button emits `documents:mobile-pages` (nothing opens yet; the Pages sheet is a later ticket). `viewer.setChrome( { info, pages } )` is how `mobile.js` tells the viewer which round buttons the footer cannot show.
-

**Rotation fade:** when the size starts changing, the pages (mobile frame and edge hints, or the desktop book) fade out in 120 ms; once the size has been still for 250 ms the layout is rebuilt and the pages fade in over 260 ms after they are drawn (at most 1.5 s wait); none with reduced motion. No fold shading in a mobile spread.
- **Desktop dialog on touch** (tablets, unfolded foldables: wider than 700px and taller than 520px): StPageFlip's mouse and corner events are off under `(pointer: coarse)`; `viewer.js` binds swipe (`onSwipe` in `view.js` calls `turn()`), pinch and double-tap to the book, and picks one or two pages with `spreadFits()`. Mouse desktops keep `stepFor()` (always a spread when portrait).
